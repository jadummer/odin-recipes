A Healthy Breakfast

A healthy breakfast is a great way to start the day. My favorite is yogurt with fruit and granola.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup of whole milk yogurt or greek yorgurt
  • 1/4 cup of your favorite fruits, or anything seasonal
  • 1/4 cup of granola
  • Bonus: 1/2 tbsp of chia seeds
  • Bonus: A drizzle of honey or maple syrup

Steps

  1. Cut up any fruit you want to include.
  2. Assemble the yogurt, fruit and granola in layers to be fancy.
  3. Add chia seeds for protein and honey or maple syrup for a sweeter taste.
