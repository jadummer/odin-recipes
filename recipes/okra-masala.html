Okra Masala

Okra masala is a delicous curry that is easy to make. I grow okra in my garden, which has a beautiful flower as well. This recipe is adapted from one of my favorite cookbooks, Vegetarian India by Madhur Jaffrey.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of okra pods
  • 5 tablespoons of oil
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp of minced ginger
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 can of diced tomatoes, drained with a little liquid set aside
  • 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • 3/4 tsp of chili powder
  • 2 tbsp fenugreek leaves

Steps

  1. Clean the okra pods and cut them into 1/2 inch pieces.
  2. Heat 3 tbsp oil over medium-high heat and add okra when hot. Fry until lightly brown. Then turn heat down a little and saute for about 15 minutes.
  3. Salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Remove the okra from the pan, then add the rest of the oil and turn heat back to medium-high.
  5. Cook onions about 5 minutes, then add ginger and garlic, lowering the heat to medium. Fry for another minute until fragrant.
  6. Add tomatoes, set-aside juice, and spices and cook until sauce thickens.
  7. Add the okra back in adding more salt to taste.
