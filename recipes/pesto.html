Pesto

Pesto is a delicous and simple sauce made from just a handful of fresh ingredients. If you grown your own basil, this is a wonderful way to use it.

Ingredients

  • Basil (2 cups)
  • Parmesan cheese (3/4 cup)
  • Pine nuts (1/4 cup)
  • Extra virgin olive oil (3/4 cup)

Steps

  1. Mix all the ingredients together in a food processor or blender, except the olive oil.
  2. Once blended well, continue to blend while adding the oil slowly.
  3. Taste and add more of any ingredient to taste.
